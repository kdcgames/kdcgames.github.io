Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-28

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile games published by Kdc Games (e.g. Candy Sort Puzzle). By using our apps you agree to this policy.

Data we collect

Our games do not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email, or contacts. Through third-party SDKs the games collect, in aggregate:

  • Usage & gameplay analytics (levels played/completed, session info, approximate device model/OS) — via GameAnalytics.
  • Advertising data (advertising identifier, ad interactions, coarse device info) — via Google AdMob, to show ads.

How we use it

To operate and improve the games, understand engagement, and display advertising.

Third parties

Data is processed by these providers under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • GameAnalytics — gameanalytics.com/privacy

Children

Our games are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
